# AKUEBplus

### Making AKU-EB learning more accessible.

AKUEBplus is an educational platform built to help students prepare for AKU-EB examinations with organized learning resources and SLO-based study materials.

Our goal is to make exam preparation more accessible, structured, and convenient for students.

🌐 **Website:** https://akuebplus.com

## What We Offer

- 📚 Educational resources for AKU-EB students
- 📝 Past papers and examination preparation materials
- 🎯 SLO-based learning resources
- 📖 Subject-specific study materials
- 🎓 Resources for secondary and higher secondary students

## Our Mission

To make quality educational resources easier to access and help students prepare for examinations with greater confidence.
